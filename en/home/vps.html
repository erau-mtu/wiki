VPS

For members of the ERAÜ (Estonian Radio Amateurs' Union), we offer a free private server in the public 44net (AMPRNet) 44.30.195.100 - 44.30.195.200 address space. This means you have your own personal, permanent server that is directly and publicly accessible from the internet—to be used for projects, experiments, and services related to amateur radio. For example, a radio club website.

The service is intended exclusively for ERAÜ members and solely for amateur radio-related use.

A new Radio Communications Networks Committee begins operations within ERAÜ


Over the years, repeaters and communications networks have been built in Estonia through voluntary efforts, but their maintenance, coordination, and development have so far remained fragmented. The goal of the new committee is to bring this work under one umbrella and encourage all amateur radio operators to use repeaters and communication networks more actively. Our objective is simple: more airtime, more coverage, and more amateur radio operators on the air every day!

What does the committee do?
  • Maintenance and coordination of analog and DMR repeaters across Estonia
  • IP networks and IP radio links
  • HF digital systems operating on shortwave
  • LoRa networks
  • ERAÜ cyber security
  • ARDC grants and 44net (AMPRNet) interfaces
  • Supporting crisis radio communications through infrastructure
  • Partnerships, including with Internet Service Providers (ISPs)
  • Community growth — guides and test contacts
Joining

Any ERAÜ member interested in the topic who has paid the association's membership fee can join the committee — there are no other additional requirements. We are also looking for regional coordinators! To join, please send an email to es1krm@erau.ee.

Support the committee's work

Targeted donations are very welcome to secure financial resources for the committee! Donors can choose whether their contribution goes toward covering fixed costs or development work.

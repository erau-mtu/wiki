ERAÜ is the only nationally recognized, Estonia-wide public organization that unites and represents radio amateurs, whose members have a real opportunity to shape the future development of amateur radio as a hobby and determine its position on both the Estonian and global communications map. Therefore, by being a member of the Estonian Radio Amateurs' Union, you can "keep your finger on the pulse" and be a fully-fledged radio amateur!

The main functions of ERAÜ as an organization:

1.    ERAÜ is needed for relations with state institutions (primarily relations with the TTJA) and to ensure the growth of the amateur radio and radio communications hobby.
The state can only communicate with amateurs at an organizational level—and only a nationwide association can serve as a fully-fledged partner to the state. It is also certain that Estonian radio amateurs would not have access to the frequencies and power limits they use today if ERAÜ did not exist at this level...

2.    ERAÜ is needed for foreign contacts; we are part of the world's organized amateur community.
We must consider that we are not alone in this world, and representing Estonia with dignity in the global organization is achievable only for a nationwide association. Furthermore, according to the IARU constitution, each member country can be represented in the IARU by only one national society.

3.    ERAÜ is needed for organized domestic activities, the further development of amateur radio as a hobby, and collective work (events, gatherings, competitions).
It is important that the charm of joint activities related to our hobby does not disappear, so that everyone does not just work quietly in their own corner... The Union offers a long tradition of gatherings, the international Estonian Championships on shortwave (HF), and the "Field Day" on ultra-shortwave (VHF/UHF), as well as numerous other exciting HF and VHF/UHF competitions, a winter information seminar (Winter Day), and much more, which brings us together as colleagues and fellow hobbyists.

4.    ERAÜ is needed in the role of a communicator and information sharer (ES-QTC, website on the internet, information broadcast on the air, etc.).
It is clear that in the internet age, when information from the wider world comes home seemingly "on its own," the Union plays a decisive role specifically in distributing information and decisions related to Estonia and our field of interest. Naturally, our own published materials are also very important—this is our history, which we create ourselves for future generations. It is precisely through our own Union that we leave a mark on Estonian history and culture—let us do this as well and thoughtfully as possible.

5.    ERAÜ is needed to provide various services to its members (e.g., the QSL bureau).
Assuming that a large portion of us still want to be "classic radio amateurs"—meaning working on the air, owning and sending QSL cards, and communicating with fellow hobbyists at home and abroad—it is clear that this service provided by the Union is needed and expected. A very simple and even approximate calculation would prove that each of us individually would pay dozens of times more than what we currently pay the Union for sending/handling an equivalent amount of mail.

Every radio amateur should think about these points listed above and find the moments and reasons that are personally important to them for being a member of ERAÜ.
